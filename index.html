Why everything else fails

The heat
problem is
physics.

When ambient humidity exceeds 70%, sweat cannot evaporate. Every brand's solution relies on evaporation. In Chennai or Erode in May, that mechanism is completely dead.

COOLIR does not wick sweat. It removes heat. The distinction is the entire product.

01
Conductive heat removal

Copper-infused microfilament draws heat directly off skin — the same principle as a heat sink on a processor chip.

02
Phase change buffering

PCM yarn absorbs heat at exactly 30°C, blocking temperature rise at the skin surface for up to 4 hours.

03
Biomechanical airflow

Internal micro-channels use your footstrike energy to pump stale humid air out. Ventilation powered by walking.

04
Vascular cooling

The ankle cuff targets blood vessels near the surface — cooling blood before it recirculates to your core.

Why we exist

Winter gets
a whole industry.
Summer gets advice.

Walk into any shop and you will find thermal wear, fleece jackets, heated gloves — an entire ecosystem built to fight the cold. Summer gets told to stay inside, wear loose clothes, use the fan.

COOLIR is building the equivalent for heat. Not products that mask discomfort. Products that actively remove heat from the body using phase change materials, conductive yarn physics, and biomechanical ventilation — made for India's climate.

Every product has one primary engineering target: 3°C body temperature reduction. Breathability, odour control, UV protection — all necessary, none sufficient. The temperature target is the product.
